01 / About
01

Curiosity is
the beginning.

I am Ansh Srivastava, a Class 11 student and JEE aspirant with a strong interest in science, technology, space research and innovation.

My goal is not simply to learn existing ideas, but to understand how things work, question them, experiment with possibilities and gradually transform those ideas into projects.

This portfolio represents that journey — the subjects I explore, the projects I work on, the questions I investigate and the ideas I want to develop in the future.

02 / Community & Innovation
ASTROVYN

Converting curiosity into innovations.

ASTROVYN is an evolving space where ideas, research, experiments and technology come together. It is built around a simple principle: curiosity should not stop at questions — it should lead toward exploration, experimentation and innovation.


Through ASTROVYN, different concepts can be researched, discussed, developed and eventually presented to the world. The long-term vision is to create a platform where young minds can explore science and technology beyond conventional boundaries.
